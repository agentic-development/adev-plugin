## Intent

fix(diagnose): a slow producer must not suppress the success verdict

`adev/diagnostic-slow` fires when a producer exceeds a 200 ms SOFT
timing budget. It carries `severity: 'info'` because it reports a
property of the MACHINE — how loaded it was — not of the artifacts under
inspection. lib/lifecycle-state.mjs already excludes it from
`diagnostic_warnings` on exactly that basis ("observability-only
severity").

`formatHuman` did not make the distinction. Any entry in
`envelope.errors` suppressed the "All checks passed." line, so under
load a clean project rendered as:

  [registry-error] adev/diagnostic-slow: runner exceeded 200 ms soft
                   budget: adev/status-enum-legal (took 250 ms)

with no verdict line at all. A wall-clock threshold was leaking into the
program's semantic output.

That is the whole of the reported "test isolation" flake (issue-593):
tests/cli/diagnose.test.mjs passed 29/29 alone and failed in the full
suite because concurrency pushed a producer past 200 ms. It was never
shared state — the original failure text, recovered from the run that
first exhibited it, names the slow budget explicitly.

Info-severity registry entries are now still printed (the observability
is worth keeping) but no longer suppress the verdict, and are dropped
under --quiet to match how info-severity firings are already treated.
Error- and warning-severity entries are unaffected.

`formatHuman` is exported so the renderer can be driven with a synthetic
envelope; the tests are deterministic rather than trying to make a
machine slow on demand. Verified to fail 2/6 against the pre-fix
renderer and pass 6/6 after. Full suite green at 5127 tests.
