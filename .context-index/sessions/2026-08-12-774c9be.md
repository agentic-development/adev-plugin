## Intent

docs(research): correct the skill-compression claim — its inputs are broken too

The study recorded tests/evals/skill-compression/ as "inert only because
its outputs/ tree was never captured", and named it the highest-leverage
existing asset for Phase 1. The missing outputs are real; "only" was
wrong.

Measured by md5 and line count across the variant matrix:

  brainstorm  baseline 298  conservative 216  aggressive 179
              summarized IDENTICAL md5 to baseline
  specify     baseline 682  conservative 564  aggressive 107
              summarized IDENTICAL md5 to baseline
  plan        baseline 969  summarized 1002 (LARGER than baseline)
              conservative MISSING   aggressive MISSING

Of 12 matrix cells only 7 hold a genuinely distinct variant file: two are
byte-identical duplicates of baseline, one is incoherent (a "summarized"
variant larger than the original), and two do not exist.

Capturing outputs against this matrix would produce a
summarized-vs-baseline comparison guaranteed to score identically — not
because compression preserved quality, but because the two files are the
same bytes. That is worse than no data: it reads as evidence that
summarising is free, which is exactly the conclusion the harness exists
to test.

Root cause of the miss, recorded as a method note: this study audited
whether each harness RAN. It did not audit whether each harness's INPUTS
were valid. Those are distinct checks, and the second is where this hid.

Repair sequenced ahead of output capture as epic-108 (issue-620 repair,
issue-621 capture, issue-622 CI wiring, dependency-ordered).
