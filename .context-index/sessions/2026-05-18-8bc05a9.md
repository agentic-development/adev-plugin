## Intent

feat(output-personas): wire session-start hook to concatenate persona + verbosity

The session-start hook now resolves both persona and verbosity from
the same flat-config hierarchy (local > global > per-persona default),
loads the persona directive AND the verbosity overlay, and concatenates
them with a blank-line separator before injecting via additionalContext.
Verbosity is validated at parse time (enum + path-traversal denylist)
matching the lib/persona.mjs validation contract. Missing overlay
template degrades to persona-only directive; the verbosity dir absence
also degrades cleanly. Hook always exits 0 (Hook protocol compliance,
SEC-4 review finding).

Adds two hook-level tests: persona+overlay concatenation in the
default-config case, and degrade-to-persona-only when the overlay dir
is absent.
