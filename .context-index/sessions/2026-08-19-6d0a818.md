## Intent

refactor(skills): adopt spec layout for prose-referenced companions

Move the 27 companion files that are referenced only from adev's own
SKILL.md prose into the spec-sanctioned subdirectories:

  references/  mode files, reviewer/researcher prompts, the eval rubric
  scripts/     write-test's detect-framework / detect-gaming / write-handoff

The 18 files addressed by the `plugin:<skill>/<path>` URI contract stay
where they are: skills/review-specs/*-prompt.md, review-specs/adapters/,
and validate/checks/. Those URIs are materialized into every user
project's governance/review.yaml and validate.yaml, so relocating them
breaks installed projects at review time -- and since they already live
outside SKILL.md, moving them would reclaim no body tokens.

Provider mirrors under providers/{codex,opencode}/skills/ carried their
own root-level copies; those are moved in step so the mirrored SKILL.md
prose still resolves.
