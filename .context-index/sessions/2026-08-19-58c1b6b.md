    behind a conditional-loading pointer it would stop being unconditional.

    the conditional-loading pointer

    mode files number their own steps 3/4/5, so offsets taken across the
    concatenation would compare headings from different modes

audit now walks references/ (a SKILL.md-only walk would have found zero
and degenerated into [] === []), and the cursor adapter test now reads the
published tree, pinning that companions are published at all.
