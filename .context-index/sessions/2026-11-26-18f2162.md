## Intent

feat(implementation): exempt exact provider-mirror regenerations from the lite tripwire

When a lite task edits a skill file, regenerating its providers/codex and
providers/opencode mirrors no longer trips the final-pass scope-mismatch
check. A mirror touch is exempt only when the canonical skill file is
declared or touched by the same task, the mirror's stored blob equals the
sync output generated from the canonical's stored bytes, its mode matches
the canonical's, and it is a regular file; a deleted mirror needs a
deleted canonical. The exemption never applies to the sensitive-path or
boundary legs and fails closed on any doubt. The mirror transform moves to
lib/sync/provider-skills.mjs, shared by the sync script and the tripwire;
the sync now propagates the canonical's executable bit, validates
--provider and --root, and refuses to write through a symlink.

Operator decision (2026-11-26) to close the EV-5 lite-share gap; ADR 0020
records it for the revision-4 amendment of BEH-7.
