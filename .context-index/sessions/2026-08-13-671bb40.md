## Intent

docs(test-strategies): review round 1 — BLOCK on all four revision-5 specs

Runs /adev:review-specs at full rigor (structural-architect,
security-reviewer, consistency-analyzer) over the four new non-code
strategy specs. Consolidated verdict BLOCK on all four; statuses moved
review-pending -> review-blocked. Adds .review.md reports and
.blockers.md sidecars with canonical blocker_ids, plus the reviewer and
step lifecycle events.

Substantive blockers found:
- SA-1/SA-2: the declared detection-cascade positions are unsatisfiable.
  detectTaskStrategy is a fixed linear rule list; snapshot asks to sit
  after visual AND before fixture, tolerance after threshold AND before
  fixture. Neither is reachable without reordering shipped rules.
- SA-3: all three profiles assert "all 8 contract fields load without
  fallback" but never specify seed_data_rule or permitted_tools;
  profiles.mjs falls back to unit on an empty permitted_tools array.
- SA-4: the post-hoc-baseline gaming detector has no data substrate —
  the proposed strategy_verification payload carries no capture
  timestamp or baseline checksum.
- SEC-1: capture/read/measurement commands are recorded verbatim in the
  handoff block with no env-var-only rule, so an inline connection
  string would land in a committed artifact.
- SEC-2: operator_deferred's "recorded source" is not bound to the
  hook-injected Author-type/Operator provenance, so an agent could
  self-attest a human deferral — reproducing the 235-of-391 silent-skip
  failure under a new label.
- CON-1: INVALID_SPEC_PATH collides with the specify assertWithin
  hard-failure contract; needs LEDGER_SPEC_PATH_UNRESOLVED.

The two central design decisions survived review. The structural
architect explicitly endorsed reusing the lifecycle event log as the
status ledger ("sound and the reasoning is complete") and confirmed
tolerance is genuinely distinct from threshold rather than redundant.
