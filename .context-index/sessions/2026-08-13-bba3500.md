## Intent

test(issues): de-flake the stale-takeover claimed_at assertion

CI failed on "hands a stale claim to the next claimant with a fresh
claimed_at" while the same job passed on rerun. The assertion compared the
takeover's stamp against the ORIGINAL claim's, and those two writes are
milliseconds apart — on a fast runner they land in the same millisecond and
the ISO strings are byte-identical.

The property that actually matters is that a takeover does not INHERIT the
expired lease. That stamp is 120 minutes old by construction, so comparing
against it is deterministic. The <5-minute freshness check already covers
"the new stamp is current".
