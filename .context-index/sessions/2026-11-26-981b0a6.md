## Intent

feat(implementation): invert the review-depth grant to quick-by-default

The implement review depth now baselines at quick unless a floor fires:
--tier full stays absolute, --tier quick or the spec's policy value sets
the baseline (policy lite reads as quick until the lite grant lands), and
an absent value defaults to quick. The floor raises to full on risk-level
(high-risk and not routed auto-agent), routing human-only, boundary,
sensitive-path (declared paths, plus touched paths on the final pass) and
critical-finding. The quick-grant predicate and the batched-task and
scope-mismatch legs are removed; every final pass needs a diff range.

Review depth: quick synthesized review by operator override (resolver
returned full, sensitive-path floor). Final-pass depth not recorded: the
task ran in a parallel worktree.
