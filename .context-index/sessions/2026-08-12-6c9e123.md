## Intent

docs(pr-review-brief): fix slot-1 rationale in pr-body-advisories

The spec justified putting the size advisory in slot 1 as "the only
section that changes what a reviewer does before opening the diff", but
review-packet-template puts the marker pair after all four author sections
and pins the closing marker as the last non-blank line — so in a rendered
PR the advisory sits below the packet, not at the top. Both contracts hold
simultaneously, which is why the consistency pass missed it; the rationale
was still wrong.

Keeps the placement and corrects the reasoning. Author packet first is
right on orientation grounds (an attention map means little before ## What
says what the change is for) and is a safety property: a cicd delivery bug
that replaces from the opening marker to end-of-body destroys only
generated content when the block is last. States explicitly that "slot 1"
means first among the five generated sections, not first in the PR body.
