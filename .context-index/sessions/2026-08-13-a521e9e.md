## Intent

docs(test-strategies): charter rev 5 + four specs for non-code test strategies

Extends the test-strategies charter from 9 code-shaped strategies to 12,
adding first-class support for projects whose deliverable is not code
(issue-558, from the 2026-08-10 audit).

New Live Specs (all review-pending, charter-revision 5):
- snapshot-strategy-profile: checksummed pre-change baseline + declared
  delta, for emitted/derived state. Distinct from fixture (baseline is
  captured from a live system, not authored) and from visual (textual /
  structured artifacts, never image baselines).
- reconciliation-strategy-profile: two independently-produced populations
  compared across three mandatory legs (count, schema, profile) at a
  declared grain, with a closed divergence classification set.
- tolerance-strategy-profile: data-quality bands with declared, justified,
  immutable numeric tolerances. threshold stays performance-only; the two
  strategies never overlap on a detection path pattern.
- verification-ledger-and-deferred-state: closed four-value outcome enum
  (red/green/deferred/error, no "skipped") recorded on the EXISTING
  lifecycle event log. No new results store.

Two design decisions of record, both recorded in the charter:

1. The lifecycle event log IS the status ledger. appendEvent/currentState/
   renderMarkdown already give append-only per-task history and a rendered
   board; plan_task and test_depth_assigned establish the per-task keying
   precedent. A proposed strategy_verification canonical event is marked
   [BOUNDARY: human-approved] per ADR-0009 and is NOT landed by these specs.

2. "Deferred for credentials" is never a pass. deferred is a distinct,
   non-passing outcome carrying a closed reason_code and the specific unmet
   infra_requirements entry sourced from runPreflight(). Agents may not
   choose operator_deferred. A task with an assignment and no outcome
   reports unverified. This closes the 235-of-391 silent-skip failure.

prompt/LLM-output is deferred, not omitted: /adev:eval already ships an
LLM-as-Judge layer, so it is an overlap rather than a gap.

Specs only — no lib/, hook, or SKILL.md changes. write-test, implement,
validate, and hygiene wiring is named as required follow-up in each Module
Impact Map and deliberately left unedited (contended surfaces).
