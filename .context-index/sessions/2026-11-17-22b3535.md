## Intent

fix(implement): give every step its own heading and pointer in the SKILL body

The progressive-disclosure split extracted the whole
`## Repo-Mode-Inside-Workspace Advisory` H2 into a companion, and Steps
1.5 through 4 were H3 children of that H2. The body therefore reached the
per-task loop, integration gate, final review and completion only through
a pointer summarized as "the one-line advisory printed when running
repo-scoped inside a workspace", which an agent outside a workspace has
every reason to skip. That violated BEH-3 of the progressive-disclosure
spec (the body keeps every section's heading).

Steps 1.5-4 now sit under `## Process` with their own headings and
pointers; repo-mode-advisory.md holds only the advisory, loaded only when
detectWorkspace(cwd) is non-null. The deepest pointer chain drops from 3
hops to 2.
