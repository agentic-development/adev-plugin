## Intent

docs(test-strategies): test-depth-policy spec through rev 4 + ADR-0016

Adds a test depth policy: configurable test granularity (per-task /
per-behavior / per-spec) and depth (minimal / standard / thorough), with
routing complexity able to escalate depth upward but never reduce it, and
a safety floor that cannot be narrowed.

Motivated by a three-repo audit (adev-plugin, alteryx_migration, ambev
accelerator) which found that the per-task test-file mandate in
skills/plan/SKILL.md produces append-only test growth with no
consolidation pressure, and that test volume tracks task granularity
rather than the risk or complexity of the change.

ADR-0016 settles where depth resolves and how the assignment persists:
granularity at plan time from static config, depth at test-authoring time
owned by /adev:implement, assignments as append-only lifecycle events
rather than a new sidecar (ADR-0012's four-peer set stays closed).

Four full-tier review rounds are recorded in the lifecycle log; blockers
went 8 -> 7 -> 6 -> 4. The spec is review-blocked at revision 4 with four
open blockers documented in the blockers sidecar.
