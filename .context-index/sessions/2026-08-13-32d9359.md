## Intent

docs(maintenance): hygiene test-debt spec rev 3 — anchor classification for Class B refs

Self-found during dogfood recon, not from review. A census of the real suite shows
readFileSync(join(VAR, "literal")) splits into repo-anchored constants (141 DOCS_DIR,
26 PLUGIN_ROOT, 12 SW_DIR, 12 FIXTURE_DIR) and temp-dir variables (52 root, 42 dir,
18 tmp). Rev 2's literal-only restriction would have dropped the first group with the
second, re-breaking the SA-1 coverage it was meant to fix.

Class B now classifies the path anchor (repo-anchored / temp-anchored / unresolvable)
by a same-file const-binding scan. DEAD_TEST_REFERENCE — the only detector asserting a
negative — consumes repo-anchored refs only; APPEND_CHAIN and PROSE_ASSERTION also take
unresolvable ones, where a mis-grouping costs nothing.
