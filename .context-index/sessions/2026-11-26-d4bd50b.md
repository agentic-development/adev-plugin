## Intent

feat(implementation): exclude lite tasks from batches with --lite and --run-base

adev implement batches reads governance at --run-base and keeps every task
that would be granted lite out of a batch (solo, reason lite). A sequential
group is split at its lite members into contiguous runs, each gated and
sized on its own, so plan order holds; batches carry a segment number. The
verb accepts --tier full|quick|lite so an explicit --tier quick|full opts
out of a policy lite, refuses --lite with --tier full|quick, and requires
--run-base whenever lite can be authorized. An unresolvable spec assumes
authorization (solo, reason lite-assumed, LITE_AUTHORIZATION_ASSUMED), and
the verb writes no lifecycle event.
