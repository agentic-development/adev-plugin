## Intent

fix(artifact): reject reports whose frontmatter is not first

The second producer of malformed artifacts, after PR #221 fixed the spec
templates. Reviews and validations have NO template — they are written by
the agent and committed through `adev artifact commit` — so the fix
belongs at that choke point rather than in a template.

Scale: 122 of 123 `.validate.md` and 153 of 202 `.review.md` files in
this repo open with a heading or comment above the `---` delimiter. That
is the larger half of epic-104 (~275 files versus 128 specs).

Prose alone had already failed. `/adev:validate` ALREADY routes through
this verb; the rename simply never inspected content. Per
constitution.md:68 a check that gates the next step belongs in the CLI,
not in skill prose — so `adev artifact commit` now refuses with
ARTIFACT_FRONTMATTER_NOT_FIRST and leaves the `.tmp` in place to fix and
re-run.

Why it matters beyond a quiet diagnostic: `adev/frontmatter-present` is
severity `error`, and `lib/specify-revise.mjs` cannot parse an artifact
whose frontmatter is not first — the same defect that made `adev specify
revise` unusable on template-authored specs.

`hasLeadingFrontmatter` mirrors `lib/diagnostics/tier1/
frontmatter-present.mjs` exactly (leading blank lines tolerated, any body
before `---` rejected) so the writer and the diagnostic cannot drift.

Prose updated in both skills, and the codex/opencode mirrors regenerated
via scripts/sync-provider-skills.mjs. Note `/adev:review-specs` does NOT
currently route through the verb, so for reviews the prose is the only
lever today — routing it through `adev artifact commit` is worth a
follow-up.

Four existing artifact tests and one integration test carried plain-text
fixtures that the new contract rejects; their bodies now lead with
frontmatter. Those assert rename semantics, not content shape, so the
fixture update is the correct fix rather than a weakened check.

Tests: tests/cli/artifact-frontmatter-first.test.mjs — heading-first and
HTML-comment-first rejected (the comment case is the `.partial` marker
shape from issue-517), frontmatter-first published, leading blanks
tolerated, review kind covered, and the zero-byte guard still fires
first. Verified to fail 3/7 against the pre-fix verb and pass 7/7 after.
Full suite green at 5147 tests.
