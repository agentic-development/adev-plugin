## Intent

docs(validation): ADR 0011 — source-manifest re-stamping authority

Proposes that /adev:validate may re-stamp the source-manifest block in a
spec's frontmatter on operator opt-in when validation passes against the
current code, while /adev:hygiene stays advisory and /adev:implement retains
exclusive authority over initial stamping.

Resolves the ambiguity in spec-lifecycle/charter.md ("recomputable by any
skill") versus source-manifest.spec.md (only describes /adev:implement
writing). Without this rule, drift flags can only be cleared by re-running
the full implementation lifecycle — a poor fit for legitimate small edits
to manifest-tracked files.

Open questions explicitly deferred: spec-side vs code-side drift precedence,
host portability when hooks don't run, session-summary manifest snapshots.
