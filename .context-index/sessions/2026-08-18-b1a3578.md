open with six architectural blockers. Operator decision: keep the software
bundled domain exactly as it is today, no changes.

software rather than by anything else in the initiative:

  wrong — the distinction does not exist in the code.

- installDomainProfile writes `extends: ${parent}` unconditionally and cannot
  express a root domain.
- The upgrade trigger was unowned.

  <projectRoot>/.context-index/domains/; the two never meet.
- It contradicts template-resolution.spec.md Behaviors 3 and 4.

extensions under <pluginRoot>/extensions/, so Phase A's web-service domain works
as a vendored first-party extension exactly as data-engineering does today, and
loadDomainConfig reads project-local installs before bundled. Phases 1-3 and A-C
are unaffected.

Removed from Business Intent, Scope, Target State, Dependencies, Migration Plan
and Acceptance Criteria. Dependencies downgraded: domain-profiles and
