## Intent

fix(strategic-planning): don't abort the whole auto-retry cycle on one malformed finding

Root-caused via the real convergence eval: treatment#1 halted after round 1
(cycles=0, no revise attempted) even though 4 of its blockers were
perfectly well-formed and auto-retry-eligible. The Wiring Reviewer emitted
6 findings as free-form prose instead of the structured format, and
/adev:review-specs correctly excluded just those 6 from .blockers.md
(logging LEGACY_REVIEWER_OUTPUT per finding) -- but skills/build/
blocker-auto-retry-loop.md's step 3 treated ANY LEGACY_REVIEWER_OUTPUT
marker anywhere in .review.md as grounds to abort the entire cycle,
discarding the 4 usable blockers along with the 6 unusable ones.

skills/review-specs/SKILL.md's own aggregator docs already scope the
exclusion to the single malformed finding; only its downstream-consequence
claim ("no /adev:specify --revise dispatch occurs") was wrong -- that's a
per-finding exclusion, not a whole-cycle abort. Fixed both: the build
loop's step 3 now checks whether .blockers.md is actually empty (a true
"every finding this round was unusable" case) rather than re-deriving this
from .review.md, and review-specs' cross-reference to the downstream
consequence matches. Synced provider mirrors.
