## Intent

fix(build): dispatch optimistically; do not introspect tool availability

The orchestrator was self-aborting pipeline steps after scanning the
loaded tool list / deferred-tools list for "Agent" and concluding the
dispatcher was unavailable. Agent is eagerly loaded (not deferred), so
its absence from ToolSearch is the expected state — not evidence of
unavailability. The harness is the only authority on tool availability:
the orchestrator must attempt Agent({...}) and let the harness reject
if absent.

Adds a Dispatch Optimism subsection under Delegation Protocol, two new
Red Flags entries forbidding pre-dispatch introspection and self-abort
on tool-list absence, and a regression smoke test asserting the prose
invariants are preserved.
