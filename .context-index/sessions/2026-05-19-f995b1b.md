## Intent

feat(retro): classify uncommitted artifacts as durable vs transient

The /adev:retro skill only inspected committed history, so durable framework
artifacts left uncommitted by their writers (lifecycle-state JSONL, session
summaries from .githooks/post-commit, drift stamps from lib/spec-drift.mjs)
were either invisible to the retro or misclassified as transient operational
state.

Add a Step 1.8 working-tree scan with a path-pattern classifier table, an
Uncommitted Artifact Health analysis in Step 2, and class-specific
recommendations in Step 3 that route durable artifacts to /adev:reconcile
and transient files to .gitignore. Red Flags now forbids lumping the two
together and forbids treating a `drift_detected:` frontmatter diff as a
real spec edit.

Follow-up issue-523 (in shared task board) tracks moving the classifier
table out of skills/retro/SKILL.md into manifest.yaml so /adev:hygiene and
/adev:reconcile can consult the same source.
