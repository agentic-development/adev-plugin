## Intent

docs(test-strategies): positional context-family discriminator (round-9 fix)

SA-49/SEC-35: the round-8 fix excluded any Task-N heading whose remainder
contained "Context" as a substring, which misclassified 14 shipped task
BODIES whose titles legitimately contain the word ("### Task 1:
Context-pack library", "### Task 2: Prototype Context Reception",
"scanContextGaps()"), losing their path leg and — worse — silently
widening the previous task's region across them.

The discriminator is now positional and case-sensitive: a heading is
context-family iff its remainder after "Task <N>" matches
^(\s*[-–]\s*\d+)?\s+Context\b — the word in the position the
context-packet template puts it, with an optional range suffix. Verified
programmatically against all 14 corpus cases in both directions before
committing: every context-packet form matches, every body-title collision
is admitted. New AC pins the title-collision case. Also stated that
readTaskFiles' `available` boolean maps onto the floor_inputs enum
(CON-54).
