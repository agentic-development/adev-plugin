feat(strategic-planning): add DECISION_REQUIRED/EXTERNAL_REMEDY loop exits and inner/outer retry cap separation

finding_class BEFORE dispatching authoring (via adev specify
group-blockers's new decision_blocker_ids/external_blockers fields):
decision halts immediately with DECISION_REQUIRED, no authoring ever
dispatched; external is excluded from convergence accounting and
renders an "External remedies" progress line (via
lib/governance/remedy-ref-render.mjs, shared with Task 11's review
report channel), while the loop continues on remaining defect
blockers.

on an unresolved referent, constructs a new mechanism-existence
blocker and loops back to authoring within a fixed 3-attempt inner
counter tracked independently of retries_remaining -- exhausting it
stops BUDGET_EXHAUSTED without touching the outer counter, and vice
versa.

blocker_count_history/not_converging_window and adds NOT_CONVERGING
-> LOOP_NOT_CONVERGING to the verdict-action table.

Full loop mechanics extracted to a conditional-loading companion file
(skills/build/blocker-auto-retry-loop.md) to stay under the Copilot
SKILL.md size cap (was already at ~100%).

Task 6's real-diff change broke (it asserted the pre-fix blanket-
acknowledgement behavior) -- now passes authoredSections matching
real heading anchors, and adds coverage for the finding_class
branches, the inner/outer cap independence, and NOT_CONVERGING.
